Mini restoration

Union Jack restores classic Minis — Austin and Morris Mini, Cooper and Cooper S — in San Martin, California. On a Mini the subframes, floors, A-panels and heelboard usually decide whether the job is a repair or a full rebuild.

What we take on

Minis look small and they are, but the structure is unforgiving. Front and rear subframes carry the suspension; the monocoque around them rusts in predictable places. We repair or replace what has gone, then put the geometry back properly.

Every project starts the same way: we look at the car, then write an estimate and a stage-by-stage worksheet before any work begins.

What usually needs doing

Honest notes on where these cars go wrong, so you know what to look for before you buy one or bring it in.

  • Front and rear subframesThe Mini hangs its suspension on detachable subframes. Rotten towers, cracked mounts and distorted frames upset geometry and tyre wear. They can be repaired or replaced; guessing from outside the car is a false economy.
  • Floors and seat basesCabin floors and the seat-base pressings trap moisture. Once they go soft, seat belts and seat mounts have nothing honest to bolt to.
  • A-panels and door stepsThe A-panel (outer and inner) and the step take splash from the front wheels. Cover sills and filler over soft metal are common on cars for sale and expensive to undo.
  • Heelboard and rear valenceThe heelboard ties the rear structure together behind the seats. Combined with a rotten rear subframe mount, it is often why a Mini feels loose at the back.

Mini questions

Straight answers, including the ones about cost.

Are Mini subframes structural?

They carry the suspension and locate the powertrain. A sound shell with rotten subframe mounts still needs proper repair before geometry and braking will feel right. We assess both the shell and the frames before quoting.

What usually rusts first on a classic Mini?

Floors, A-panels, door steps, heelboard and the subframe mounts. Sills and the battery tray are close behind. A dry-looking paint job tells you almost nothing about those areas.

What does a Mini restoration cost?

It depends on how far the structure has gone. A solid car needing mechanical work and paint is a different job from one needing floors, A-panels and both subframes. We assess the car and give a written estimate with a worksheet.

Do you work on Cooper and Cooper S models?

Yes. Cooper and Cooper S cars need the same structural honesty as a standard Mini, plus careful attention to the engine, gearbox and brakes that make them what they are.
