Our restoration process — from intake to road test

Union Jack Ltd. restores British cars in San Martin, California through a documented sequence: assessment and estimate, media blast and body work, paint in our booth, mechanical and electrical rebuilds, upholstery and trim, then a final road test before delivery. Owners stay informed with photos and updates. Call (408) 686-1101 to start.

13555 Depot Ave, San Martin, CA 95046 · (408) 686-1101

1. Assessment & estimate

Bring the car — or clear photos — to 13555 Depot Ave. We inspect structure, rust, mechanicals, and prior work, then discuss goals: Concours d'Elegance contender, reliable tourer, or partial refresh. You receive a written estimate before major work begins.

2. Disassembly & documentation

We photograph and label assemblies as we take the car apart so parts can be reused, refurbished, or correctly replaced. This step protects originality where it matters.

3. Media blast & body

On full restorations we media-blast to bare metal, repair rust in floors, sills, and outriggers, and straighten panels before primer. Structural integrity comes before cosmetics.

4. Paint

Color is sprayed in our booth. We match factory shades or owner-chosen finishes, then cut and polish for a durable, even surface.

5. Mechanical & electrical

Engines, gearboxes, brakes, suspension, and wiring are rebuilt or rewired as the project scope requires — including positive-earth to negative-earth conversions when requested.

6. Trim, upholstery & final assembly

Seats, carpets, weather seals, glass, and brightwork go back on last. We road-test locally and correct issues before you collect the car.
