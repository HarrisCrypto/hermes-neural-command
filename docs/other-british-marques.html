What usually needs doing

Honest notes on where these cars go wrong, so you know what to look for before you buy one or bring it in.

  • Daimler SP250 is glassfibre on a chassisThe body is glassfibre over a steel chassis, with Edward Turner's 2.5 litre V8 behind it. Early A-specification cars are known for chassis flex, and glassfibre repair is a different discipline from panel beating.
  • Riley RM bodies are timber framedSteel panels over an ash frame, in the traditional way. As with the MG T-series, decayed timber means the body loses its shape and the repair is joinery as much as metalwork.
  • Morgan frames are ash tooA Morgan's body frame is ash on a steel chassis. Frames can be replaced, and doing it is often the honest answer on a car that has been damp for years.
  • Parts are the long poleFor rarer cars the search for correct parts drives the schedule more than the labour does. We do that search before giving a figure, so the estimate reflects what can actually be obtained.

Other marques questions

Straight answers, including the ones about cost.

Do you restore Daimler SP250s?

Yes. The SP250 has a glassfibre body on a steel chassis with a 2.5 litre V8. Glassfibre repair is a separate skill from metalwork, and early A-specification cars are known for chassis flex that is worth assessing first.

Can you work on cars with ash body frames?

Yes. Riley RM saloons, Morgans and the MG T-series all use timber framing under steel or aluminium panels. Frames can be spliced or remade; that work is joinery as much as it is panel beating.

What if parts for my car are hard to find?

That is normal for rarer marques and it is why we search for parts before quoting rather than after starting. Thirty-eight years of contacts, including suppliers in the UK, is what makes an honest schedule possible.

Do you take on Rolls-Royce and Bentley work?

Yes, along with Lotus and Land Rover. Tell us what the car is and what you want from it and we will say honestly whether we are the right shop for it.
