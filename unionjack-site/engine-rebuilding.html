Engine rebuilding for British classics

Union Jack Ltd. rebuilds British car engines in San Martin, California — from inspection and machine work through careful assembly and break-in guidance. We work on Jaguar, MG, Triumph, Austin-Healey, Mini, Morris Minor, and related powerplants as part of full restorations or standalone engine-out projects. Call (408) 686-1101 to discuss your engine.

What engine work includes

Typical scope covers teardown inspection, measurements, machine work as needed, new bearings and seals, carburetion or injection sorting, and test running. Exact parts and machine operations depend on the engine family and condition.

Standalone or full restoration

Engines can be rebuilt as a single job or as one phase of a full restoration. We will tell you honestly when a block or head is beyond economical repair.
