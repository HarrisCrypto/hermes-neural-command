Where classic Minis usually need steel

Union Jack restores classic Minis in San Martin, California. Before we quote one, we walk the structure in a fixed order: subframes, floors, A-panels, heelboard. Paint condition is almost irrelevant at this stage.

Subframes first

Front and rear subframes carry the suspension. Cracked mounts, rotten towers and distorted frames show up as odd tyre wear and a car that will not hold an alignment. The frames unbolt; that does not make them optional.

Floors and A-panels

Cabin floors and seat bases trap moisture. A-panels and door steps take wheel splash. Cover sills over soft metal are common on cars for sale and expensive to put right later.

Heelboard

The heelboard ties the rear structure behind the seats. Together with tired rear subframe mounts, it is often why a Mini feels loose at the back even when the engine bay looks tidy.

None of this is a claim about one customer car. It is the pattern we see across the model. When a specific project is ready to publish with verified facts, it will get its own dated entry.
