Frequently asked questions

Owners ask the same questions before a British car restoration: how long it takes, whether we work on non-British cars, if visits are allowed, how parts are sourced, and what an estimate includes. Here are clear answers from Union Jack Ltd. in San Martin, California — call (408) 686-1101 for anything specific to your car.

How long does a full British car restoration take?

A complete restoration at Union Jack Ltd. typically takes 12–24 months depending on rust, parts availability, and whether the goal is Concours d'Elegance or a reliable tourer. Partial projects finish sooner. Call (408) 686-1101 for a timeline on your car.

Do you work on cars that are not British?

Yes. While British marques are the core of the shop, Union Jack Ltd. also restores select American classics and other interesting projects when capacity allows. Contact the shop to discuss fit.

Can I visit the shop during a restoration?

Yes. Visits are by appointment at 13555 Depot Ave, San Martin, CA so we can walk you through progress without interrupting spray or welding work. Call (408) 686-1101 to schedule.

Do you source parts for older British cars?

Yes. We source OEM, NOS, and quality aftermarket parts for Jaguar, MG, Triumph, Austin-Healey, Mini, Morris Minor, and related British marques, and we can fabricate when catalog parts are unavailable.

What does a restoration estimate include?

Estimates outline body and paint scope, mechanical work, electrical, upholstery, and known parts. Rust discoveries can change the number; we update you before expanding the job.

Where is Union Jack Ltd. located?

Union Jack Ltd. is at 13555 Depot Ave, San Martin, CA 95046, south of San Jose in Santa Clara County. Phone (408) 686-1101.
