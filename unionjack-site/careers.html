Careers at Union Jack Ltd.

Union Jack Ltd. is hiring skilled tradespeople for British car restoration in San Martin, California — body and metal fabrication, paint, mechanical assembly, and related shop roles. Experience with Jaguar, MG, Triumph, or similar classics is valued; a strong work ethic and pride in finish quality matter most. Email a résumé to info@unionjack.com or call (408) 686-1101.

13555 Depot Ave, San Martin, CA 95046 · (408) 686-1101

Who we are looking for

  • Body / metal fabricators comfortable with rust repair, panel beating, and welding
  • Painters experienced with classic automotive finishes and booth discipline
  • Mechanical assemblers who can rebuild engines, brakes, and suspension carefully
  • General shop talent willing to learn British-car specifics on the job

How to apply

Email info@unionjack.com with a short note about your experience and a résumé or work photos. You can also call (408) 686-1101 and ask for John or Marcello. Confirm the mailbox is monitored before relying solely on email.
